[h2]Numeric caps[/h2]
Factory output, production efficiency, construction speed, intel decryption, research speed and special project speed are all capped, defaulting to the Standard tier at +1000%.
Switch between four tiers in the "Value Tuning" category: Loose +10000%, Standard +1000%, Strict +100%, or pick "Unlimited" to lift the cap.
The same page has ten fine-tune steps, from ±1% to ±10000%, so you can land on whatever number you want.
Production efficiency hits the game's own hard ceiling at +10000%; anything higher has no effect.
Unlimited lets factory output and production efficiency feed each other; the numbers will explode and ruin the save. Choose with care.
Point-based values such as research points, manpower and MIO funds are not capped.

Production items have been restructured into a category + production line toggle structure.
The AI will not use this mod's features.

Compatible version: HOI4 1.20.*
