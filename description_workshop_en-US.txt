[h2]Numeric caps[/h2]
Factory output, production efficiency, construction speed, intel decryption, research speed and special project speed are all capped, defaulting to the Loose tier at +200000%.
Switch between five tiers in "Production Line Management": Loose, Standard, Strict, Very Strict, or pick "Unlimited" to lift the cap.
Unlimited lets factory output and production efficiency feed each other; the numbers will explode and ruin the save. Choose with care.
Point-based values such as research points, manpower and MIO funds are not capped.

Production items have been restructured into a category + production line toggle structure.
The AI will not use this mod's features.

Compatible version: HOI4 1.20.*
